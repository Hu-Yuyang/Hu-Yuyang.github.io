Core contributor to **<a href="https://gemini.google/overview/video-generation/">Gemini Omni</a>**, Google's multimodal model for video generation and editing. Omni turns any combination of text, images, and video into a generated clip, and lets users refine the result through natural-language editing. Announced at Google I/O 2026.
